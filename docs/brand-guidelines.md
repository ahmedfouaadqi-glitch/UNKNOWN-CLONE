# UNKNOWN brand guidelines

## Name
- English: UNKNOWN.
- Arabic: غير معروف.
- Working lockup: UNKNOWN. / غير معروف.
- Arabic tagline: قل ما بداخلك، دون أن تكشف من أنت.
- English tagline: Say what you feel. Stay unnamed.

Trademark and domain availability must be cleared before commercial launch.

## Visual language
- Monochrome graphite pencil / line-art illustrations.
- White daytime background, black nighttime background.
- No neon, 3D, photographic stock art or colorful decorative gradients.
- Reuse the same original icon family across navigation, empty states, categories and app surfaces.
- Quiet interaction; respect reduced-motion settings.

## Logo assets
- `public/brand/unknown-mark.svg`: transparent logo symbol.
- `public/brand/unknown-mark-dark.svg`: light-stroke version for dark surfaces.
- `public/brand/unknown-wordmark.svg`: horizontal lockup artwork.
- `public/brand/unknown-favicon.svg`: browser favicon.
- `public/brand/unknown-icon.svg`: installable icon artwork.

## Responsive
Mobile-first; dedicated breakpoints for compact phones, phones, tablets, laptops and wide desktops. Components cannot rely on hover alone; touch targets and keyboard focus remain available.
